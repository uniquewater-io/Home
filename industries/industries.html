Dosing Pump Solutions

Types of Dosing Pumps For Industrial Applications

Different pump technologies are selected according to chemical characteristics, operating pressure, required capacity, accuracy and process conditions.

Mechanical Diaphragm Dosing Pumps

Mechanical diaphragm metering pumps provide controlled chemical dosing through a reciprocating diaphragm mechanism.

  • Water treatment
  • Chemical processing
  • Industrial utilities

Hydraulic Diaphragm Dosing Pumps

Hydraulic diaphragm pumps use hydraulic actuation to deliver metered chemicals in demanding process applications.

  • High-pressure dosing
  • Chemical plants
  • Process industries

Packed Plunger Dosing Pumps

Reciprocating plunger pumps provide metered liquid delivery for applications requiring suitable pressure and flow characteristics.

  • Industrial chemical dosing
  • Process injection
  • High-pressure applications

Solenoid Operated Dosing Pumps

Compact electronically controlled pumps suitable for controlled chemical injection in smaller water treatment systems.

  • RO plants
  • Water purification
  • Small dosing systems

Electronic & Digital Metering Pumps

Electronically controlled dosing pumps offer adjustable operating settings and can support automated process control arrangements.

  • Automatic dosing
  • Process control
  • Water treatment

Complete Chemical Dosing Systems

Engineered assemblies combine dosing pumps, tanks, mixers, pipework, valves and instrumentation according to application requirements.

  • Chemical preparation skids
  • Dosing panels
  • Multiple chemical systems
